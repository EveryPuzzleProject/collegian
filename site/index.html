Mike Shenk's crosswords in The Daily Collegian, 1976–1981

Mike Shenk, who later edited the Wall Street Journal crossword, was a Penn State student when the Collegian started running his daily puzzle; the first with his byline is 12 March 1976. The archive has no issues from December 1975 to March 1976, so the true start of the run falls somewhere in that gap and can't be pinned down. In June 1981 the paper ran a help-wanted ad in the puzzle's place: he had left for GAMES magazine. We've found which page the crossword is on in every issue the archive holds, the first step to preserving the puzzles and making them searchable as xd files.

The Collegian is in copyright, so this page holds no images. Every square links to the issue in the Pennsylvania Newspaper Archive, which is open to all, with page images. More in the blog post.

Exceptions

  • 22 March 1976, p. 4
    A puzzle titled "Nazism" whose black squares form a swastika, with clues on Nazi Germany. It will be kept, under its title, and flagged when it's imported.
  • 19 January 1978
    No crossword was printed; the next day's paper apologized and reprinted the grid.
  • 20 January 1978, p. 1
    The front page reprints the grid left out of the day before, with no clues ("We can't think of anything else you could possibly need"). The issue's regular puzzle is on page 4.
  • 10 June 1981, p. 2, 12 June 1981, p. 2, 15 June 1981, p. 2, 17 June 1981, p. 2, 19 June 1981, p. 2, 22 June 1981, p. 2, 24 June 1981, p. 2, 26 June 1981, p. 2, 29 June 1981, p. 2
    A help-wanted ad in the puzzle's place: Mike Shenk "has secured a job with GAMES Magazine" and the paper is looking for "two or three bright, witty persons to take his place".
